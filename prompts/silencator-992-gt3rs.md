# SILENCATOR — 992 GT3 RS film (prompt pack)

Format: **16:9**, 10 shots × 3 s = **30 s**. The structure follows the reference reel:
the car leaves home at golden hour, detail shots follow, it drives at dusk and blue hour, and the film ends at night with a fade to black.

Generate **each shot as its own clip** (5–8 s) and save it as `clips/01.mp4` … `clips/10.mp4`.
The edit uses the best 3 seconds of each clip.

---

## Car block (paste at the start of EVERY prompt)

> A dark forest-green Porsche 911 GT3 RS, current 992 generation: large swan-neck rear wing,
> louvred vents on top of the front fenders, big central hood vents, side fender fins, shark-fin
> roof spine, satin black center-lock wheels, glossy deep forest-green paint. Black license plate
> with white characters reading "SILENCATOR" on the front and rear. The car is identical in every
> shot. Photorealistic, shot on cinema camera, natural film grain, soft contrast, warm highlights
> and deep green shadows. Beverly Hills: a modern luxury home with warm interior lights and quiet
> residential streets lined with tall hedges and palms.

## Negative prompt (every shot)

> text other than the license plate, watermark, captions, people, other cars, burnout, tire smoke,
> drifting, exaggerated revving, 991-style wing, wrong car color, CGI look, video-game look,
> oversaturated colors, warped wheels, morphing bodywork

> [car block] Wide static shot. The Porsche idles just inside the open gate of a modern

### Tips
- **Plate text:** video models often garble it. For shots 05 and 08, make a still first
  in an image tool that renders text well, then animate it with image-to-video.
- **Consistency:** reuse the same car still as an image reference for every shot if your tool allows it.
- Generate 2–3 versions of key shots (01, 05, 08, 09) and keep the best.
